Professional Summary

I am a Bachelor of Pharmacy student at the Jamia Hamdard, New Delhi, with academic training in computational drug design, research methodology, and analytical chemistry.

My work centres on molecular docking, high-performance liquid chromatography, and the pharmacology of natural products. I have authored a published literature review on the nutraceutical potential of Tinospora cordifolia, presented ethnopharmacology research at the SECON 2025 international convention, and completed certified training in Good Clinical Practice.

I read primary pharmacology literature closely, evaluate safety and toxicity data, and report findings in clear scientific writing. I am now seeking research-focused postgraduate study or an industry internship in drug discovery.
